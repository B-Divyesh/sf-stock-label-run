Terms for barcode label runs

Stock Label Run turns your receiving data into printable barcode labels and a run receipt. You remain responsible for the data, barcode rights, printer setup, and scanner requirements.

Supported use

The app checks EAN-13 and UPC-A length and checksum, Code 39 characters, and printable-ASCII Code 128 subset B. A passing check means only that the value can be encoded by this app. It is not barcode allocation, GS1 registration, marketplace certification, inventory management, or a guarantee that a scanner or retailer will accept the result.

One-time upgrade

The optional Run room upgrade costs US $12 once. It includes unlimited labels per run, additional stock sizes, and custom label footers. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record. Checkout is temporarily unavailable while product registration completes. When active, payment, taxes, receipts, and refunds are handled there. A refund revokes the associated license. Accessibility, CSV checking, A4 label output, receipt history, and data export remain available without purchase.

Your data and backups

Files and receipts are held locally in the browser. Browser clearing, device loss, or private-browsing expiry can remove them. Use the included JSON export for backups. We cannot restore data that never left your device.

Warranty and liability

The software is provided “as is,” without warranties. Test a small sheet on your exact label stock and scan it before committing a full run. To the extent permitted by law, the authors are not liable for stock loss, mislabeling, print waste, or downstream decisions based on the output.
