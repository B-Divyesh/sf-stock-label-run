Plain-language policy · 28 August 2026

Privacy stays on the receiving desk.

Stock Label Run is local-first. Your purchase lists, product names, SKUs, barcodes, draft, and print receipts stay in your browser’s IndexedDB. We do not receive or analyze them.

What leaves your device

Nothing during the free label workflow. If you buy or restore the one-time unlock, checkout opens on Sociobot’s hosted billing service. The license token is sent to api.sociobot.in for verification at most once per day. Payment details are handled by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, and never pass through this app.

Storage and control

The app stores your current CSV, settings, receipt archive, and any license token in this browser. Use “Export backup” to take your receipt history with you. Use your browser’s site-data controls to erase everything. Deleting an individual receipt is also available in the app.

Offline cache

A service worker caches the app shell, illustration, and legal pages so the utility can open offline. It contains no tracking code, advertising SDKs, remote fonts, or third-party runtime scripts.

Questions

Email privacy@sociobot.in. Please do not send CSV files or license tokens by email.
