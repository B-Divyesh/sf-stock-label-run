Privacy policy · 6 September 2026

How Stock Label Run handles data

Your purchase lists, product names, SKUs, barcodes, draft, and print receipts stay in your browser’s IndexedDB. The free workflow does not send them to us.

What leaves your device

Nothing during the free label workflow. If you restore a one-time upgrade license, the token is sent to api.sociobot.in for verification at most once per day. Payment details are handled by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, and never pass through this app.

Storage and control

The app stores your current CSV, settings, receipt archive, and any license token in this browser. Use “Export backup” to take receipt history with you. Use your browser’s site-data controls to erase everything. You can also delete an individual receipt in the app.

Offline cache

A service worker caches the app shell, illustration, and legal pages so the utility can open offline. It contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, remote fonts, or third-party runtime scripts.

Questions

Email privacy@sociobot.in. Do not send CSV files or license tokens by email.
